    • 🌎 Cyclone Safety

    Know what to do before, during and after an cyclone.

    A cyclone is a powerful rotating storm with strong winds and heavy rainfall that can cause flooding and damage.


    Safety Tips

    • Stay indoors and remain calm.
    • Keep doors and windows closed.
    • Stay away from windows and glass.
    • Keep emergency supplies ready.
    • Follow official weather warnings and evacuation orders.
