Know What To Do

Every disaster is different. Understanding the correct response can help you stay calm and make safer decisions. Explore the guides below to learn basic preparedness steps.

NATURAL DISASTER

🌍 Earthquake

An earthquake is sudden ground shaking caused by movement beneath the Earth's surface.

Remember

  • Drop, Cover and Hold.
  • Stay away from windows.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • Move to a safe open area after shaking stops.

WATER HAZARD

🌊 Flood

A flood occurs when water covers normally dry land due to heavy rainfall, overflowing rivers or storms.

Remember

  • Move to higher ground.
  • Never walk through flood water.
  • Never drive through flooded roads.
  • Follow official warnings.
Open Flood Guide →
FIRE HAZARD

🔥 Fire

A fire can spread quickly and produce dangerous heat and smoke.

Remember

  • Stay calm and raise the alarm.
  • Move away from smoke and flames.
  • Use stairs instead of elevators.
  • Do not return to a burning building.
Open Fire Guide →
WEATHER HAZARD

🌪️ Cyclone

A cyclone is a powerful rotating storm that can bring strong winds, heavy rainfall and flooding.

Remember

  • Stay indoors during dangerous conditions.
  • Keep doors and windows closed.
  • Stay away from windows and glass.
  • Follow official weather warnings.
