🛡️ About EduSafe

Building awareness for safer communities.

Our Mission

EduSafe aims to provide simple and accessible disaster preparedness information for students, families and communities.


What EduSafe Provides

  • Disaster safety guides
  • Emergency preparedness checklist
  • Emergency contact information
  • Basic disaster response instructions
  • Emergency alarm feature

Our Goal

Our goal is to encourage people to prepare before a disaster happens and understand the correct actions to take during an emergency.
