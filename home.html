About EduSafe

EduSafe is a simple and educational disaster preparedness platform created to provide useful safety information in one place.

From earthquakes and floods to fires and cyclones, EduSafe explains what people can do before, during and after an emergency.

Our goal is to make disaster preparedness easier to understand, especially for students and young learners.

Know the Disaster

Learn about common disasters and understand the basic safety actions that can help protect you and your family.

🌍 Earthquake

Sudden shaking of the ground caused by movement beneath the Earth's surface.
