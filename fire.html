🌎 Fire Safety

Know what to do before, during and after an fire.

What is an Flood?

A fire is a rapid burning process that produces heat and light, which can spread quickly and cause damage.


Safety Tips

  • Stay calm and raise the alarm.
  • Move away from the fire and smoke.
  • Use stairs instead of elevators.
  • Stay low if there is smoke.
  • Call emergency services and do not go back inside.
