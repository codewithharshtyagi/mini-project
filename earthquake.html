🌎 Earthquake Safety

Know what to do before, during and after an earthquake.

What is an Earthquake?

An earthquake is a sudden shaking of the ground caused by movement beneath the Earth's surface.


Safety Tips

  • Stay calm.
  • Drop, Cover and Hold.
  • Stay away from windows.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • After shaking stops, move to a safe open area.
